About

A little about me

I've been building for the web since 2005 — long before React was a thing, and long before "micro frontend" was even a term anyone used.

Most of those years have been at Gupshup, a conversational messaging platform. I joined as a Web Developer and slowly grew into a Lead Software Engineer role — which meant moving from jQuery and JSP to modern React applications and Micro Frontend architectures. It's a journey I'm genuinely proud of.

I've shipped messaging platforms, campaign management consoles, and wallet applications that real businesses use every day. The thing I care about most is the whole picture: understanding what's needed, building it thoughtfully, and making sure it keeps working well in production.

These days I also spend a good chunk of my time mentoring junior developers on the team — something I find really rewarding. I like helping people grow the same way I was able to.
